INVENTORYDRIFT Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Information We Process

INVENTORYDRIFT may process your Google account identity, inventory records, product information, sales, stock movements, and application settings to provide inventory management services.

Google Account and Drive

When you authorize Google, the application uses Google OAuth and the Google Drive API to access files permitted by your granted permissions. Inventory data may be stored in your Google Drive in accordance with the application's configuration.

Use of Information

Information is used to operate inventory features, synchronize authorized data, maintain application functionality, and troubleshoot errors.

Storage and Security

Data stored in Google Drive is subject to Google's services and your account settings. Some information may also be cached locally on your devices for offline use. Protect access to your devices and Google account.

Sharing and Retention

Access to inventory files depends on your Drive permissions and sharing settings. Files may remain in Drive until you delete them. Local cached data may need to be cleared separately.

Disconnecting Access

You can revoke the application's Google access through your Google Account security settings. Revoking access may stop synchronization but does not necessarily delete files already stored in Drive.
